# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Kokos to be a practical, respectful, and useful project for people who
run Telegram communities. Contributors and users are expected to keep
discussion professional and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful when discussing bugs, feature requests, and moderation concerns.
- Share enough context to reproduce issues.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Respect platform rules, copyright, and the privacy of other users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Publishing private credentials, tokens, chat exports, or user data.
- Encouraging abuse of Telegram, TikTok, YouTube, or hosting providers.
- Using project spaces to coordinate spam, harassment, scraping, or copyright abuse.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or restrict
participation when behavior harms the project or its users.
